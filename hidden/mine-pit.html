Mine Pit

A prototype. Blast an open pit into the hillside and sell the ore. The iron seam shows at the surface and dips into the hill. The water table sits four metres below the surface. Below it, groundwater seeps into the pit. A flooded pit cannot be blasted, so place pumps to keep it dry.

  • Every charge costs money. Ore in the blasted rock pays it back.
  • Surface water flows as a shallow water simulation on the graphics card.
  • Groundwater is a second layer. Pumping lowers the water table around the pit.
  • A pump lifts water at most twelve metres. Deeper pits need a chain of pumps.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
