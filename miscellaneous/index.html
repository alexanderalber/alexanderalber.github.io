Miscellaneous

Tools & visualizations

All tools here run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent, stored or tracked.

3D Modelling (3)
  • Terrain Erosion Simulator Simulate hydraulic erosion on a heightmap, like the erosion nodes in Gaea: rain droplets carve gullies and sediment fans, with live 3D before/after preview and 16 bit PNG export - entirely in your browser.
  • Heightmap to STL Turn a grayscale heightmap (8 or 16 bit PNG) into watertight STL terrain tiles for 3D printing, with 3D preview and anti-warping options - entirely in your browser, the file never leaves your device.
  • Trophy Generator Design a personal trophy as a gift: pick a shape and a base, engrave a dedication, preview in 3D and export a print-ready STL - or mount your own STL on the base. Runs entirely in your browser.
World Modelling (1)
  • A Model of Psychohistory Posterior predictive futures of a galactic civilization - a coupled stochastic model with priors, interventions, credible bands and a few hundred thousand world-lines.
Language Modelling (2)
  • Token & Character Counter Paste text and count tokens, characters, words, sentences, lines and bytes live - with the o200k_base tokenizer (GPT-4o / o-series) and a colored token view, entirely in your browser.
  • Claude Code settings.json Generator Click together a Claude Code settings.json: permission allow/ask/deny rules, model, environment variables and defaults. Copy or download, all in your browser.
For Parents (8)
  • Name Finder Browse and filter hundreds of german baby names.
  • Parental Leave Planner A month-by-month grid covering 24 life months, letting both parents independently assign Basis, Plus, or Partnerschaftsbonus months by clicking cells. Calculates total payout and more.
  • Pregnancy Timeline Maps pregnancy symptoms over pregnancy weeks. Also risk curves for miscarriage probability and premature birth survival.
  • Child Development Timeline Visualizes developmental milestones across domains. Overlaid with WHO percentile growth charts for weight and height.
  • Honest Due Date Calculator Models the birth date as a skew-normal distribution with an evidence-based expected due date, based on multiple factors.
  • Birth Decisions Explorer Explore the choices around birth: selecting an option live-locks incompatible ones and explains why. Quantified effects, an evidence traffic-light per statement, and a generated birth-plan draft for the midwife conversation.
  • Infographic: Baby Sleep, Feeding & Crying Total sleep and longest continuous night stretch, feeding frequency (breast vs. formula), and crying and fussing duration per day.
  • Infographic: Daycare Infections Infections per year and sick days per year, shown as P10–P90 bands.
Books (2)
  • Book Recommender Rate the fantasy & sci-fi books you know and get recommendations with calibrated probabilities, a taste map and much more.
  • E-Book Converter Convert EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and plain text into EPUB, MOBI or plain text - entirely in your browser, the file never leaves your device.
Other (6)
  • Image Editor Crop, scale, rotate, draw, add text and warp the four corners - built on a non-destructive operation stack you can reorder and roll back like a CAD history. Entirely in your browser, the image never leaves your device.
  • Input Shaping Calculator Driving a damped spring-mass system without residual vibration: ZV/ZVD/ZVDD/EI shapers, step response and sensitivity curve.
  • Drug Concentration Visualiser Pharmacokinetic blood-level curves: drag intake times, combine doses and immediate/extended-release forms, and watch steady-state accumulation.
  • Race Time Predictor Enter your running PBs (10K, marathon, …) and get predictions for every other distance - Riegel formula with a personally fitted fatigue exponent.
  • Sing Trainer Practice hitting the right notes: scrolling sheet music, live pitch detection from your microphone, generated melodies, scales and hold-the-note drills for every voice type.
  • Subway Sauce Guide Which sauce goes with which Subway sandwich? Heatmap with switchable color scale.
