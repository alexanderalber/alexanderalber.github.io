Miscellaneous

Things that run in your browser

Small tools, grouped by topic below, almost all of them built with Claude Code; the ideas, the maths and the responsibility for the mistakes are ours. None of them asks you for anything: every "Sign in to continue" on the web costs a little soul, and soul does not grow back.

All tools here run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent, stored or tracked.

Nothing here matches that.

Off the list:

Making Things (7)
  • Terrain Erosion Simulator Simulate hydraulic erosion on a grayscale heightmap. Rain droplets carve gullies and sediment fans. Live 3D before/after preview and 16 bit PNG export.
  • Heightmap to STL Turns a grayscale heightmap (8 or 16 bit PNG) into watertight STL terrain tiles for 3D printing. With 3D preview and options against warping.
  • Trophy SculptorEN Generates 3D-printable trophies as STL. Set the height, pick one of thirteen families (lattice, flame, twisted stele, quartz cluster, bismuth crystals, gnarled tree, power transformer, cup, slit cone, flying wing, F-22, a counterexample lasso, a cube graph) and engrave the plinth. Every piece prints upright without support material, in one colour or two.
  • Sign MakerEN Builds a flat 3D-printed sign: a rectangular, oval or cloud-shaped head with raised lettering and a frame, on a spike, a band or an octagonal rod for sticking into a cake. The raised parts print in a second colour after one filament change.
  • Print NestEN Upload an STL and get a transport case that fits it. Choose two halves that close around the model, a cover block or a draped shell. The clearance is adjustable, and every variant prints without support.
  • QR Code GeneratorEN Generates QR codes with free colours, a transparent background and PNG or SVG export. The Anatomy view shows which modules carry data and which carry redundancy.
  • PCB Art DesignerEN Turns a picture into a decorative circuit board with LEDs behind translucent windows. Each area gets a material: bare FR4, soldermask, exposed copper or silkscreen. Export order-ready Gerber and Excellon files or an editable KiCad project.
Language Modelling (7)
  • Token & Character Counter Counts tokens, characters, words, sentences, lines and bytes as you type. Two tokenizers are available: o200k_base (GPT-4o, o-series) and cl100k_base (GPT-3.5, GPT-4). A colored view marks each token.
  • Claude Code settings.json GeneratorEN Click together a settings.json for Claude Code. Set permission rules (allow, ask, deny), path rules for the scope you pick, model, environment variables and defaults.
  • System Prompt Builder Click together a personal system prompt for your chatbot. Pick a variant for each aspect or leave the aspect out, and edit any block. Copy the result as Markdown, in English or German.
  • Local LLM FrontierEN Which is the best language model your GPU can run? Set VRAM, context length and KV cache precision. The tool shows which open-weight models fit, about how fast they run and which closed frontier model they roughly match.
  • LLM Detector Paste English or German text and get a calibrated probability that a language model wrote it. Typical AI phrases are highlighted, and the features behind the score are shown.
  • Language Zipper Paste a few words in any language, and a compression model guesses which language it is. You also get a family tree of 87 languages, built from the same measurements.
  • Text CorruptorEN Zalgo text along a curve: draw how corrupted the text gets from first character to last. Fourteen effects land on whole clauses, more often where the curve is high.
Models & Uncertainty (5)
  • Quap LabEN Write a Bayesian model as a formula list and load your data as CSV or JSON. The tool fits the model by quadratic approximation or Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (NUTS). Follows McElreath's Statistical Rethinking.
  • Input Shaping Calculator Drives a damped spring-mass system without residual vibration. Compare ZV, MZV, ZVD, ZVDD and EI shapers by step response and sensitivity curve, then copy the matching Klipper configuration.
  • Drug Concentration Visualiser Shows the blood level of a drug over the day. Drag intake times and combine doses and forms (immediate or extended release). Over several days the level builds up to steady state.
  • Leverage & RuinEN How much leverage is too much? A Monte Carlo simulation of a leveraged ETF shows volatility drag, the Kelly optimum, break-even leverage and the probability of a wipe-out.
  • Race Time Predictor Enter your running PBs (5K, 10K, marathon, …) and get predicted times for every distance from 1 km to 50 km. The Riegel formula uses a fatigue exponent fitted to your own times.
Playground (7)
  • A Model of PsychohistoryExperimentalEN Simulates possible futures of a galactic civilization with a coupled stochastic model. Set priors and schedule interventions, then watch the credible bands of the long-run trajectory shift.
  • Ruler Title FinderEN You are newly in power and need a title. Seven questions score 44 historical ruler titles for fit. Each title comes with its real history.
  • Grabby Aliens SimulatorENExperimental A 3D simulation of the grabby aliens model. Expanding civilizations fill the universe and block later ones from appearing. The numbers show how early humanity is.
  • Lambda Diagrams Type a lambda-calculus term and see it drawn as a Tromp diagram. Beta reduction then runs step by step, and every step is animated. Church numerals, combinators and your own terms.
  • Ripple TankEN A live wave simulation in full screen. Drop in slits, lenses and prisms or paint walls freehand. Diffraction and refraction follow from the wave equation.
  • Vortex StreetEN A wind tunnel on your GPU. Put a cylinder, an airfoil or your own drawing into the flow and watch the vortex street form. Drag, lift and Strouhal number are measured as it runs.
  • Reaction DiffusionEN A Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion simulation in full screen: two chemicals, four numbers. Drag across the feed/kill map, and the pattern changes from solitons to worms to dividing cells.
Games (2)
  • Flow Field DefenseENExperimental A maze tower defense that draws the cost field the enemies walk on. Layers under the board show cost to go, threat coverage and where the enemies will actually walk.
  • HikyakuEN A ring race against the clock through a procedurally generated cyberpunk city at night: neon, holograms, rain and dense air traffic. The route runs down a street canyon and through a hollow block. Then it enters an arcology: a pyramid with a ventilation duct and turning fans, or a block you dive down through, storey by storey. Fly a courier glider with a 3D mouse or the keyboard.
For Parents (8)
  • Name Finder Browse hundreds of German baby names. Filter by origin, sound, syllables and popularity trend, and see each name's rank in Germany since the 1980s.
  • Parental Leave Planner for Germany (Elterngeld) A grid over the child's first 24 months: both parents assign Basis, ElterngeldPlus or Partnerschaftsbonus. The total payout accounts for income tax (Progressionsvorbehalt). A Pareto front shows what months at home cost at least.
  • Pregnancy Timeline Shows pregnancy symptoms week by week: when nausea, fatigue and other complaints start, peak and fade. Risk curves show the probability of miscarriage and survival after premature birth.
  • Child Development Timeline Shows a child's developmental milestones across several domains. The WHO percentile growth charts for weight and height are overlaid.
  • Honest Due Date Calculator Shows how likely the birth is on each day, as a skew-normal distribution. The due date comes from last period, ultrasound or transfer. Earlier births, height and other factors from studies adjust it.
  • Birth Decisions ExplorerExperimental Choose your preferences for the birth, from epidural to place of birth. Incompatible options lock immediately, with the reason. Every effect is given as a number with its evidence rating.
  • Infographic: Baby Sleep, Feeding & Crying Distribution curves for the first year of life: total sleep, longest night stretch, feeding frequency (breast or formula), and crying and fussing per day.
  • Infographic: Daycare Infections Infections and sick days per year over the daycare years, for a daycare child and a child cared for at home. Shown as P10–P90 bands.
Books (2)
  • Book RecommenderEN Rate the fantasy & sci-fi books you know. You get recommendations with the probability that you will like each one, plus a taste map.
  • E-Book ConverterEN Converts EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, Markdown, HTML and plain text into nine formats. Convert one file, a whole batch, or merge several books into one.
Images (9)
  • Background RemoverEN Removes a white or single-coloured background in your browser. White areas inside the subject stay. An AI mode decides what belongs to the subject.
  • Image EditorEN Crop, scale, rotate, draw, add text and straighten a slanted facade. Every edit stays a step in a list that you can reorder or undo.
  • Painting SimulatorEN Paint with pigments instead of pixels. Colors mix by Kubelka-Munk optics: blue and yellow make green. The brush runs out of paint.
  • Color ExtractorEN Shows which colors a photo or logo is made of. You get eight palettes from two to nine colors, each color with its share of the image. Copy them as hex, CSS or JSON.
  • Color GradingEN Grade a photo in the browser with color wheels, curves, HSL secondaries, split toning and live scopes. A separate tab develops Sony ARW raw files. Export PNG, JPEG or a .cube LUT.
  • Patch Similarity ExplorerEN Click a spot in an image, and a heatmap shows how similar every other region is. The comparison uses DINOv3 features from a 15 MB model that runs in your browser.
  • Pixel to VectorEN Converts a PNG, JPEG or WebP into a clean SVG in your browser. Black-and-white tracing works with subpixel accuracy. Color mode stacks one layer per color.
  • Colormap DesignerEN Give it the colours a colormap has to use, and get a dozen finished colormaps to pick from: sequential, diverging or cyclic. Each suggestion comes with a damage report.
  • Pixel ForensicsEN Lays maps over a photograph that show traces the eye cannot see: the JPEG block grid, the quantisation table recovered from the pixels, noise and traces of scaling. The tool gives no verdicts.
Music (4)
  • Choir Part TrainerENExperimental Load a MusicXML or MIDI score and practise your own choir part. Your voice plays loud and the rest quiet, slower or transposed if you like. Scrolling notation and live pitch feedback from the microphone.
  • Sheet to MIDIENExperimental Upload a PDF of cleanly engraved choir music and get practice MIDI back. The music recognition runs in your browser. Play each voice on its own, export MIDI or MusicXML.
  • Sing TrainerExperimental Practise hitting the right notes. Notes scroll across a staff, and the microphone tracks your pitch live. Single notes, scales, triads and generated melodies, all within your voice range.
  • Vowel Map See which vowel you are singing. LPC estimates the first two formants live from your microphone. Your voice moves as a point through the vowel chart.
Everyday (5)
  • Subway Sauce Guide Which sauce goes with which Subway sandwich? A heatmap rates each pairing from −3 to +3, for German and US menus. The color scale is switchable.
  • Sun over Regensburg Will the sun still reach this spot at 20:15? Click anywhere on the map of Regensburg. The official surface model answers, buildings and trees included.
  • When to Close the Windows Should you shut the windows the moment it is warmer outside than inside? A thermal simulation computes the indoor peak for every combination of closing and reopening time.
  • Self-Guided Walking Tour (Budapest, Regensburg)EN A walking tour in the browser, with no app, account or signal needed. You get a site plan from OpenStreetMap, a numbered route and one text per stop. The tour saves for offline use.
  • German Marginal Tax Wedge How much of the next euro do you keep? The tool shows marginal and average burden from taxes, social insurance and benefit withdrawal over the whole income range, broken down by levy. Law as of 2026.
