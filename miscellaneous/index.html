Miscellaneous

Things that run in your browser

Small tools, grouped by topic below, almost all of them built with Claude Code; the ideas, the maths and the responsibility for the mistakes are ours. None of them asks you for anything: every "Sign in to continue" on the web costs a little soul, and soul does not grow back.

All tools here run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent, stored or tracked.

Nothing here matches that.

3D Modelling (2)
  • Terrain Erosion Simulator Simulate hydraulic erosion on a heightmap, like the erosion nodes in Gaea: rain droplets carve gullies and sediment fans, with live 3D before/after preview and 16 bit PNG export.
  • Heightmap to STL Turn a grayscale heightmap (8 or 16 bit PNG) into watertight STL terrain tiles for 3D printing, with 3D preview and anti-warping options.
Language Modelling (6)
  • Token & Character Counter Paste text and count tokens, characters, words, sentences, lines and bytes live - with the o200k_base tokenizer (GPT-4o / o-series) and a colored token view.
  • Claude Code settings.json GeneratorEN Click together a Claude Code settings.json: permission allow/ask/deny rules, Read and Edit path rules resolved against the scope you pick, model, environment variables and defaults. Copy or download the result.
  • System Prompt Builder Click together a personal system prompt for your chatbot: pick a variant per aspect or leave it out, edit any block, copy the result as Markdown - in English or German.
  • Local LLM FrontierEN What is the best language model your GPU can run? VRAM math for weights and KV cache, honest quality bands, rough speed, and the milestones of how frontier quality has been arriving on consumer cards.
  • LLM Detector Paste English or German text and get a calibrated probability that a language model wrote it - typical AI phrases marked in the text, the driving features and a Bayes factor laid open.
  • Language Zipper Paste a sentence and a character model trained on 87 languages says which one it is - plus a family tree and a map of those languages built from nothing but how cheaply each explains the others. Programming languages too.
Playground (3)
  • A Model of PsychohistoryExperimentalEN Posterior predictive futures of a galactic civilization - a coupled stochastic model with priors, interventions, credible bands and thousands of simulated world-lines.
  • Ruler Title FinderEN You are newly in power and need a title. Seven questions, a transparent fit score, and 44 historical ruler titles with real history: etymology, notable holders, occupational statistics, and how each ended.
  • Grabby Aliens SimulatorENExperimental Simulate the grabby aliens model in 3D: expanding civilizations fill the universe and suppress later ones, with honest numbers on how early humanity is, when the first wave would arrive, and why the sky still looks empty.
Games (3)
  • Flow Field DefenseENExperimental A maze tower defense that draws the field the enemies actually walk on: cost to go, threat coverage and where the crowd will really be. A tower is not a wall but an expensive cell, so sealing the map is a move with a price rather than a move the game forbids.
  • Cobra 6DOFENExperimental A wireframe Cobra flown through a gridded box with a 3D mouse, which needs a SpaceNavigator and a Chromium browser. The hull is a real rigid body: its inertia tensor is integrated over the mesh rather than guessed from a bounding box, so it rolls easily, yaws like a barn door, and tumbles about its unstable axis once a wall has grazed it.
  • Asteroid CorridorENExperimental A sandbox rather than a race: a long transparent corridor of slowly turning asteroids, flown with a 3D mouse, with no clock and nothing to reach. The rocks are collided against their own drawn surface, craters and all, rather than against a bounding sphere, and a turning rock shoves you the way a moving wall does.
Concepts, Visualised (4)
  • Lambda Diagrams Type a lambda-calculus expression, see it drawn as a Tromp diagram, and watch beta reduction morph it smoothly, step by step. Church numerals, combinators and your own terms.
  • Input Shaping Calculator Driving a damped spring-mass system without residual vibration: ZV/ZVD/ZVDD/EI shapers, step response and sensitivity curve.
  • Ripple TankEN A live wave simulation, full screen: a plane wave rolls in from the left, you drop in slits, lenses and prisms or paint walls and glass freehand, and diffraction and refraction happen because the wave equation says so.
  • Reaction DiffusionEN Two chemicals, four numbers and a full screen. Drag a cursor across the feed/kill plane and watch the pattern turn from solitons into worms into cells that divide, or paint new seeds into it with the mouse.
For Parents (8)
  • Name Finder Browse and filter hundreds of german baby names.
  • Parental Leave Planner for Germany (Elterngeld) A month-by-month grid over 24 life months where both parents assign Basis, ElterngeldPlus, Partnerschaftsbonus or unpaid months by clicking cells, with total payout, German income tax under the progression clause and a Pareto front of time against cost.
  • Pregnancy Timeline Maps pregnancy symptoms over pregnancy weeks. Also risk curves for miscarriage probability and premature birth survival.
  • Child Development Timeline Visualizes developmental milestones across domains. Overlaid with WHO percentile growth charts for weight and height.
  • Honest Due Date Calculator Models the birth date as a skew-normal distribution with an evidence-based expected due date, based on multiple factors.
  • Birth Decisions ExplorerExperimental Explore the choices around birth: selecting an option live-locks incompatible ones and explains why. Quantified effects, an evidence traffic-light per statement, and a generated birth-plan draft for the midwife conversation.
  • Infographic: Baby Sleep, Feeding & Crying Total sleep and longest continuous night stretch, feeding frequency (breast vs. formula), and crying and fussing duration per day.
  • Infographic: Daycare Infections Infections per year and sick days per year, shown as P10–P90 bands.
Books (2)
  • Book RecommenderEN Rate the fantasy & sci-fi books you know and get recommendations with calibrated probabilities, a taste map and much more.
  • E-Book ConverterEN Convert EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, Markdown, HTML and plain text into nine formats, one file or a whole batch.
Images (8)
  • Background RemoverEN Remove a white or single-coloured background entirely in your browser: flood fill keeps enclosed white areas, soft edges avoid the white fringe, and where colour is not enough, an AI mode lets a vision model decide what belongs to the subject. Export as transparent PNG.
  • Image EditorEN Crop, scale, rotate, draw, add text, pull a slanted facade straight and warp the four corners - built on a non-destructive operation stack you can reorder and roll back like a CAD history.
  • Painting SimulatorEN Paint with pigments instead of pixels: colors mix by Kubelka-Munk optics, so blue and yellow make green, pressure comes from your cursor speed, the brush runs out of paint, and every one of these inconveniences has its own off switch.
  • Color ExtractorEN Find out which colors a photo or logo is really made of, and copy them as hex, CSS or JSON: eight palettes from two to nine colors, each with its share of the image and a preview of the picture reduced to it.
  • Color GradingEN Grade a photo without an account: lift, gamma and gain on color wheels, curves, the six HSL secondaries, split toning and live scopes, all in your browser. Load a palette from the Color Extractor and pull the image toward it softly, with a strength slider and an honest distance readout. Export PNG, JPEG or a .cube 3D LUT.
  • Patch Similarity ExplorerEN Upload an image, click any spot, and see a heatmap of how similar every other region is: DINOv3 vision transformer patch features computed entirely in your browser (a 15 MB model download, no server). A second view colors the features by PCA and segments them with k-means.
  • Pixel to VectorENExperimental Convert a PNG, JPEG or WebP into a clean SVG, entirely in your browser: black-and-white tracing with subpixel accuracy on anti-aliased edges, or a color mode that quantizes to a small palette and stacks one layer per color so no seams show. One slider from exact to simple, per-layer control, honest file sizes.
  • Colormap DesignerENExperimental The published colormaps are a solved problem; the constrained case is not, where the colours are partly given to you or the quantity is an angle. Give it your colours, pick from a dozen finished sequential, diverging or cyclic suggestions, and read the damage report next to it: lightness profile, local perceptual rate, a dichromat simulation, and the five energy terms separately rather than only their sum.
Music (4)
  • Choir Part TrainerENExperimental Practise your own part from a MusicXML or MIDI score: your voice loud and the rest quiet, slower tempo, transposition, a count-in and a loop over the four bars that never work, with scrolling notation, a piano roll of all voices and live pitch feedback that also tells you when you have landed in the wrong voice.
  • Sheet to MIDIENExperimental Upload a PDF of cleanly engraved choir music and get practice MIDI back: optical music recognition entirely in your browser, per-voice playback (one voice alone, all, or one loud and the rest quiet), suspect measures flagged for proofreading, export as MIDI and MusicXML.
  • Sing TrainerExperimental Practice hitting the right notes: scrolling sheet music, live pitch detection from your microphone, generated melodies, scales and hold-the-note drills for every voice type.
  • Vowel Map See which vowel you are actually singing: the first two formants are estimated live from your microphone by LPC and your voice wanders as a point through the vowel chart. Calibrating on your own corner vowels makes two singers comparable, snapshots and a target vowel help with vowel blending in a choir.
Everything Else (10)
  • Drug Concentration Visualiser Pharmacokinetic blood-level curves: drag intake times, combine doses and immediate/extended-release forms, and watch steady-state accumulation.
  • Race Time Predictor Enter your running PBs (10K, marathon, …) and get predictions for every other distance - Riegel formula with a personally fitted fatigue exponent.
  • Subway Sauce Guide Which sauce goes with which Subway sandwich? Heatmap with switchable color scale.
  • Leverage & RuinEN How much leverage is too much? Monte Carlo simulation of a leveraged ETF over long horizons: volatility drag, the Kelly optimum, break-even leverage and wipe-out probability, fat-tailed daily returns and crash shocks that end a leveraged position in a single day. The average outcome rises without limit while the typical path peaks and turns back down.
  • When to Close the Windows Should you really shut the windows the moment it is warmer outside than in? A thermal simulation of a flat or house from nothing but the day's minimum and maximum: every combination of closing and reopening time, drawn as a field with the best one marked and the rule of thumb next to it.
  • Sun over Regensburg Will the sun still reach this spot tomorrow at 20:15? Click anywhere on the map of Regensburg and the official surface model answers for that exact point, buildings and trees included, down to a metre. Hours of sun per day and per year come as maps of their own, and the whole day can be exported as a film.
  • Meal-Prep-PlanerDEExperimental Turns a week of meals into one Sunday batch-cooking timeline, a merged shopping list and a note per dish.
  • QR Code GeneratorEN Free colours, a transparent background by default, PNG and SVG export, and no account anywhere. The error-correction slider shows which modules carry data and which redundancy, and the module shapes run from plain squares to small snakes that crawl across the code while it stays readable, downloadable as GIF or animated SVG.
  • PCB Art DesignerEN Turn an image into a light-up PCB: vectorize the artwork, paint with a material palette (bare FR4 as light windows, soldermask, exposed gold-plated copper, silkscreen), automatic back-copper clearance behind every window, and export as order-ready Gerber and Excellon files or as an editable KiCad project, entirely client-side.
  • Self-Guided Walking TourEN A walking tour that runs in the browser and needs no app, no account and no signal. The map is a site plan drawn from OpenStreetMap, the route follows the streets and stairs you actually walk, and each numbered stop carries one text written to be read while standing in front of the thing. It can be saved for offline use, and it will show your position without sending it anywhere. One tour so far: the Buda Castle District in Budapest, which is mostly a reconstruction, and the texts say which parts are not.
