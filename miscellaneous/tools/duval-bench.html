Duval Bench

Language models write the Duval triangle and the Duval pentagon from memory, without research. Both are standard diagrams for diagnosing faults in power transformers from gases dissolved in the oil. A chart shows every tested model by release date.

  • Models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and DeepSeek got the same two tasks: one JavaScript file per diagram that classifies a gas sample and draws the diagram.
  • The answers run unchanged in a sandbox. The drawings you see are the model's own.
  • The bench asks each answer for the fault zone of 10,000 gas samples and compares it with the published zones.
  • A second picture per diagram marks in red where the model's zones differ from the reference.
