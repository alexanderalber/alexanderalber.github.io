Heightmap to STL – Terrain Tiles

Turn a grayscale heightmap (e.g. from Gaea, World Machine or any DEM export) into watertight STL tiles for 3D printing: upload, choose dimensions and tile grid, preview in 3D, download a ZIP. Supports 16 bit PNG. Everything runs in your browser, your file never leaves your device.
