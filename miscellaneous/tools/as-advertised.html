As Advertised

Mobile ads keep showing a lane shooter. One soldier walks down a road, gates add to the squad, and a crowd comes down the lane. The installed game is usually something else. This page is that lane shooter.

  • Three lanes. The squad fires down the lane it stands in.
  • Enemies that reach the squad attack it, from any lane. Each blow costs a soldier.
  • Blue gates add soldiers. Shooting through a gate raises its number.
  • The squad holds 99 soldiers. Beyond that, gates make it hit harder.
  • Spike fields and steel rollers take a share of the squad that stands in their lane.
  • No ads, no shop, no account. Everything runs in your browser.
