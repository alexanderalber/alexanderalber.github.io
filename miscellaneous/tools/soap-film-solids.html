Soap Film Solids

Place a few elliptic end caps in 3D space and let a smooth skin span between them, the way a soap film would stretch across a wire frame. The result is an organic solid with flat mounting faces, exported as a watertight STL for 3D printing.

  • Each ellipse is a flat end face of the solid: position, tilt and radii are freely adjustable.
  • The skin through the ellipses minimises a curvature energy, giving soap-film-like waists between the caps.
  • A tension control trades bulgy transitions against tightly cinched ones.
  • Everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
