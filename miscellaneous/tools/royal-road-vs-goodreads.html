Every story here is among the 200 best rated on Royal Road. A low score means low within that group.

How the numbers are made

The Royal Road side is the top 200 of the "Best Rated" list on 28 October 2026. That list shows followers, but not how many people rated a story. It shows no status for about a quarter of the stories, mostly the stubbed ones; those count as "unknown".

Each story was searched on Goodreads by its title. A hit counts only when the base title matches, without subtitle or volume number. The book also has to be from 2012 or later and have at least 5 ratings. Generic titles drew only namesakes and were checked by hand. Matches that look plausible but are not certain are drawn as hollow dots. Fan fiction is not searched.

Each story uses the volume with the most ratings, almost always the first. Later volumes are rated only by readers who liked the first, so they sit higher.

A Goodreads average from few ratings is uncertain. The page offers two ways to account for that.

"Shrunk" pulls a rating with few votes towards the mean of all matches: (200 × mean + n × average) / (200 + n). A book with 20 ratings ends up close to the mean, whatever its average.

"20th percentile" treats the true rating as a distribution around the average. With fewer ratings the distribution is wider, and the page takes the value that the true rating exceeds with 80% probability. The prior here is weak (10 ratings' worth at the mean), so the book's own average counts for more. The width assumes a spread of 1 star per single rating, because the result pages do not show the spread. For a book with 20 ratings the 20th percentile lies about 0.15 stars below the central estimate, for one with 2,000 ratings 0.02 stars.

Each option of the combined score names the Goodreads estimate it uses.

The combined score works on percentiles, so the compressed Royal Road scale and the wider Goodreads scale become comparable. Both percentiles are taken among the stories found on Goodreads. The lower of the two punishes a story that only one platform loves. It also leans low, because the lower of two noisy numbers is low by chance more often.

Why the two ratings differ

Royal Road readers rate a free serial while it is still running. Readers who drop a story rarely rate it. Authors often ask for ratings in their chapter notes.

Goodreads readers rate a finished, edited, paid book. Only stories that sold well enough get a book at all, so the Goodreads side is already a selection.

Within this top 200 the two ratings correlate only weakly. Part of that is the selection: every story here is already near the top of Royal Road.
