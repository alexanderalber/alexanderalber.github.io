Self-Guided Walking Tour

A self-guided walking tour that runs entirely in your browser. The map is a site plan drawn from OpenStreetMap data, the route is a real walking line, and each numbered stop carries one text written to be read while you are standing in front of the thing rather than at home before the trip.

Your position is read on the device and never leaves it. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Location is off until you switch it on.
