Choir Part Trainer

Load the MusicXML or MIDI file of a choral piece and practise your own part against the others. Everything happens in your browser; the score never leaves your machine.

  • Per-voice mix: your part alone, your part loud and the rest quiet, or everyone but you
  • Slower tempo, transposition, a count-in and a loop over any range of bars
  • Scrolling notation for MusicXML plus a piano roll that shows all voices at once
  • Live pitch feedback from the microphone, including which voice you actually landed on
