Unlosable Minigolf

Six minigolf holes. On five of them every shot from the tee ends in the hole, whatever the direction. The walls are ellipses, circles and parabolas, and their geometry does the aiming.

  • Drag on the course to shoot. The direction does not matter.
  • Fire 180 balls in all directions at once and watch them arrive together.
  • Move the ball off the tee and see the guarantee break.
  • The sixth hole is the opposite case: it cannot be reached from the tee.
