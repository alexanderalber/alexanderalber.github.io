LLM Detector

Paste English or German text and get an estimate of how likely it was written by a language model. The detector checks stylometric signals and phrases typical of AI assistants such as ChatGPT or Claude, highlights them directly in your text and reports a calibrated probability together with a Bayes factor. The full methodology is documented on the page; the analysis runs locally in your browser and no text ever leaves your machine.
