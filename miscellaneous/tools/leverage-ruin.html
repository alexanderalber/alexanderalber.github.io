Leverage & Ruin

A leveraged ETF does exactly what it advertises: on any single day it moves twice as far as its index. Over twenty years it does something else, and the reason is not the fees. This page simulates the full range of outcomes of a leveraged position over a long horizon, and shows the average rising with leverage while the typical path peaks and turns back down.
