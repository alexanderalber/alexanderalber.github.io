Input Shaping Calculator

Shape a move command so that a damped spring-mass system stops without ringing. Compare eleven shapers by delay and robustness. For the six shapers Klipper supports, copy the matching configuration.

  • Eleven shapers: ZV, MZV, ZVD, ZVDD, EI, two- and three-hump EI, plus four with negative impulses (UM-ZV, UM-EI, PS, SNA-EI).
  • Step response, sensitivity curve and impulse sequence side by side. The shaper duration shows how much delay a more robust shaper adds.
  • The sensitivity curve shows how much vibration remains when the measured resonance frequency is off.
  • Two more modes: model inversion (feedforward) and a numerical optimiser with weighted goals.
